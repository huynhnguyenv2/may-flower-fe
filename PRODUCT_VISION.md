# Product Vision — Public Storefront (MVP)

## Brand proposition

**“A flower studio that understands people, not just flowers.”**  
Vietnamese working name context: premium custom flower studio, emotion-first gifting.

## Positioning (Vietnam launch)

- **Market:** Vietnam first (VND, delivery zones starting with major cities—see open questions).
- **Experience:** Hybrid **editorial website** + **AI Flower Concierge** (rule-based recommendations in MVP; LLM-grounded later).
- **Differentiation:** Thoughtful gifting, personalization, design quality, and clear delivery expectations—not catalog size or race-to-bottom pricing.

## Public MVP goals

1. Customers discover bouquets by **feeling and occasion**, not only category.
2. Product pages show **real offerings**, current **prices**, and **availability** from the shared database.
3. Concierge returns **Safe / Signature / Unexpected** options from the live catalog with transparent pricing.
4. Guest checkout captures recipient, message, and **scheduled delivery slot**; order is stored once for future management integration.

## Out of scope (this phase)

- Internal management UI, inventory batches, production scheduling.
- AI Operations Copilot for staff.
- Production payment integrations (stub/sandbox until provider chosen).
- Customer accounts (after guest checkout is stable).

## Success signals

- End-to-end browse → concierge → checkout on mobile.
- Availability and price on the site match API/database.
- Editorial brand feels distinct from generic ecommerce templates.
